# Body Explorer Game - User Guide

## ✅ Game is Running!

**Access the game at:** http://localhost:3004

## 🎮 How to Play

The game opens with a calm room view showing a character from a POV perspective.

### Interactive Areas:

1. **👤 Head (Top)** - Tap to explore feelings and emotions
   - Choose different faces (happy, calm, worried, excited)
   - Watch the body react with animations
   - Calm feelings trigger gentle breathing exercises

2. **💧 Underarm (Left side)** - Tap to learn about hygiene
   - See sweat droplets appear
   - Use the deodorant by clicking it
   - Watch sparkles appear when clean!

3. **📏 Body (Center)** - Tap to see growth changes
   - Swipe through 3 stages of growth
   - No text labels, just visual changes
   - See the body naturally change over time

4. **🍎 Stomach (Center-lower)** - Tap to explore food and energy
   - Choose different foods
   - Healthy foods make the body glow with energy
   - All choices are neutral (no punishment)

5. **🛏️ Bed (Background left)** - Tap to experience sleep
   - Room dims to night time
   - Character lies down
   - Stars appear, gentle sleep animation
   - Wakes up with a golden glow

6. **🪞 Mirror (Background right)** - Tap to build confidence
   - Tap to change posture
   - See confident stance appear
   - Shield icon appears showing strength

## 🎵 Audio Controls

- **Top-right corner:** 🔊/🔇 button to toggle sounds
- All sounds are soft and predictable
- No sudden loud noises

## ♿ Accessibility Features

- **Large touch targets** - Easy to tap on tablets
- **No reading required** - Visual-only interactions
- **No timers** - Take as much time as needed
- **No penalties** - Safe exploration
- **Calm animations** - No flashing or sudden movements
- **Reduced motion support** - Respects system preferences

## 📱 Device Support

- Works on tablets (recommended)
- Works on desktop browsers
- Touch and mouse supported
- Responsive design for different screen sizes

## 🎯 Design Philosophy

**"Look → Touch → Something Happens"**

The game requires ZERO instructions. Children learn through:
- Visual cues (gentle glowing on hover)
- Immediate feedback
- Predictable interactions
- Calm, game-like responses

## 💾 Progress Tracking

- Small green dots appear on visited areas
- Progress saved automatically in browser
- No visible progress bars or scores
- Just gentle encouragement to explore

## 🎨 Visual Style

- Soft pastel colors (calming)
- Rounded shapes (friendly)
- No sharp edges or busy patterns
- Single-screen focus (no navigation)
- Smooth, gentle transitions

---

**This is a GAME-FIRST experience, not an educational app.**

Children explore freely at their own pace with no pressure.
